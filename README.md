## Hi, I'm Tony 👋

I'm a transport dispatcher at a coach and minibus company. I build my own tools to take repetitive work off the desk: daily schedules, booking checks and reports for a fleet of 40+ vehicles.

### What I build
- **Excel automation with Python.** Daily and weekly reports that fill themselves from booking-system exports.
- **AI assistants with a human in the loop.** They read booking emails and prepare the changes, and nothing is applied until a person approves it.
- **Small browser tools** that need no install and print cleanly in black and white.
